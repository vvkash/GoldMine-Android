## App name

GoldMine UNCC

## Short description

Campus maps, classes, dining, discounts, UREC, and community freebies.

## Full description

GoldMine UNCC puts useful UNC Charlotte campus tools in one convenient app.

Plan your week with a simple class schedule, explore campus buildings on an interactive map,
check dining and discount information, open frequently used university resources, and view UREC
information from one home screen.

The community freebie feed lets students report items and promotions available around campus.
Reports update in real time across the Android and iOS versions, and optional notifications can
alert you when the community confirms a report.

Features include:

* Interactive campus map and building directory
* Weekly class schedule stored on your device
* Campus dining and discount information
* UREC status and university-resource shortcuts
* Real-time community freebie reports and voting
* Optional freebie notifications
* Light, dark, and system appearance settings

GoldMine UNCC is an independent student-built application. It is not affiliated with, endorsed
by, or an official product of the University of North Carolina at Charlotte.
