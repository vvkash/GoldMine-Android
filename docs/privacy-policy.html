GoldMine UNCC Privacy Policy

Effective August 28, 2026

GoldMine UNCC is an independent student-built campus companion. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official product of the University of North Carolina at Charlotte.

Overview

This policy explains the information processed by the GoldMine UNCC Android app, why it is processed, and the choices available to you.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your display name, class schedule, appearance settings, home-screen layout, notification choices, and a random app-install identifier in local Android storage. GoldMine does not upload class schedules, appearance settings, or home-screen layouts to its Firebase backend. Android backup and device transfer may copy this data according to your device settings.

Community reports and voting

Freebie reports are stored in Google Firebase Firestore. A report can contain the submitted company, selected location name and precise map coordinates, submission time, availability status, and votes. Reports are shown to other GoldMine users. Your selected display name can be stored with votes so the app can prevent duplicate voting.

Notifications and identifiers

GoldMine can store a Firebase Cloud Messaging token, random app-install identifier, display name, platform, notification preference, and update time in Firestore. This information is used to deliver optional freebie notifications and maintain notification preferences.

Location

Location permission is optional and is requested only when you choose My Location on the campus map. Google Maps may process device location to provide that feature. The app does not require live device location to create a freebie report; a report uploads the location and coordinates you select on the map.

Analytics and diagnostics

The app includes Firebase Analytics. Depending on the production Firebase configuration, Google may process app interactions, app or device identifiers, and diagnostic information to provide analytics and maintain its services. GoldMine does not sell personal information or use it for cross-app advertising.

Service providers and third-party pages

Google provides Firebase, Cloud Messaging, Firestore, Maps, and related infrastructure. The app also displays or links to university and other third-party websites in WebViews. Those services process information under their own terms and privacy policies. GoldMine does not read cookies from those sites or use them to track you across apps.

Retention and security

Community reports and notification records are retained in Firebase for app operation and moderation until they are deleted by the operator or according to backend retention practices. Network requests use encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Your choices

You may deny location or notification permission. You can turn freebie notifications off in the app's Settings and can remove the app's local data by clearing its storage or uninstalling it. Clearing local storage does not automatically delete records already sent to Firebase.

Children

GoldMine is intended for college and university communities and is not directed to children under 13.

Contact and deletion requests

To ask a privacy question or request deletion of community, notification, or install data, open a request in the GoldMine Android issue tracker. Do not post sensitive information publicly. The operator may ask you to continue through a private channel to verify and complete the request.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app or its services change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.
